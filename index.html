Metric definitions

EPA per play

Average expected-points-added value across eligible plays. Offensive EPA is from the offense's perspective; defensive EPA allowed is the opponent's EPA on plays against that defense. Higher offense is better; lower defense allowed is better.

Success rate

Share of eligible plays with EPA greater than zero. This is an explicit project definition, not a claim that all providers use the same formula.

Explosive play rate

Share of plays with a gain of at least 20 yards on a pass or 10 yards on a rush. This project's threshold is shown so rankings are interpretable.

Player EPA

Aggregated only where the play-by-play data attributes a play to a passer, rusher, or receiver. A player's total is not directly comparable across positions.
